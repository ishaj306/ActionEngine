NATIONAL MERIT SCHOLARSHIP 2026

All students of the third year are hereby informed that applications for the
National Merit Scholarship are now open.

Eligible candidates must submit the completed application form along with their
income certificate and a self-attested copy of the previous marksheet to the
designated office before 18 September 2026.

Candidates should obtain the income certificate from the Tehsildar's office.

Please verify your bank account details before submission.

A nominal fee is payable at the time of submission.
